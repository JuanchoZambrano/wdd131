Website Planning Document

DJ FROM SCRATCH - Professional Dj Academy

Site Name

DJ FROM SCRATCH: The title means building foundational skills completely from the ground up without relying on automated shortcuts.

Site Purpose

The site provides a hub for aspiring DJs to build a professional foundation by offering step-by-step basic mixing tutorials, gear and technique guidance, and direct contact for personalized support.

Scenarios

  • How can I start to learn mixing music?
  • Is it expensive to become a professional DJ?
  • Where can I learn and mix music in a professional way?
